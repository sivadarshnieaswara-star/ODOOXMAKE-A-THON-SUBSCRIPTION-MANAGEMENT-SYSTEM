# Plan 04-01: Payment Recording, Auto-Reconciliation & Overdue Invoice Tracking

## Goal
Implement payment collection and recording across multiple payment methods, auto-reconciliation of invoice balance due to Paid status, partial payment handling, and automated overdue identification and aging reports.

## Financial & Reconciliation Rules
1. **Balance Deduction**:
   - $\text{new\_balance} = \max(0.0, \text{invoice.balance\_due} - \text{payment.amount})$
   - If $\text{new\_balance} == 0.0$:
     - Transition `invoice.status = InvoiceStatus.PAID`
2. **Partial Payments**:
   - Multiple payments can be linked to the same invoice until the cumulative paid sum settles the total amount.
3. **Overdue Classification**:
   - An invoice is categorized as **Overdue** if:
     - `invoice.status == 'confirmed'`
     - `invoice.balance_due > 0`
     - `invoice.due_date < today`
   - Aging Days = $\text{today} - \text{invoice.due\_date}$

## Tasks

### Task 1: Payment Model, Sequential Numbering & Reconciliation Service
- **Files**: `backend/app/services/payment_service.py`, `backend/app/routers/payment_router.py`, `backend/app/main.py`
- **Action**:
  - Implement `generate_payment_number(db)` returning `PAY-{count:05d}`.
  - Implement `record_payment(db, invoice_id, amount, method, payment_date, note, user)`:
    - Validate that invoice exists and is not cancelled.
    - Check that payment amount does not exceed remaining `balance_due`.
    - Create `Payment` entity.
    - Decrement `invoice.balance_due`.
    - If `balance_due == 0.0`, update `invoice.status = InvoiceStatus.PAID`.
- **Endpoints**:
  - `GET /api/payments`: List payments with invoice numbers, customers, and methods (`require_staff_or_admin` or customer-filtered).
  - `POST /api/payments`: Record payment against invoice. Customers can pay their own invoices; Staff/Admin can record on behalf.
  - `GET /api/invoices/overdue`: Returns all overdue invoices with customer email, days overdue, and amount owed (`require_staff_or_admin`).
